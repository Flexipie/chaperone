Print the text stored under a hash (a `prompt_ref`, `answer_ref` or `evidence_ref` of a check event, as `check run` prints it), hashed again, not in an envelope: piped, its bytes; on a terminal, control characters other than newline and tab escaped. Refused: an unknown ref (not_found), a ref that is not 12 to 64 lowercase hex or a prefix two files share (invalid_input), a file whose bytes no longer match its name (internal). Never opens the ledger

Usage: chaperone evidence show [OPTIONS] <REF>

Arguments:
  <REF>  The sha256, or a unique prefix of at least 12 of its digits

Options:
      --db <DB>          Path to the ledger (defaults to chaperone/ledger.db in the user data dir)
      --format <FORMAT>  Output format: json is one {ok, data, meta} object; jsonl is one item per line followed by a trailer that is the only line with an `ok` key [default: json] [possible values: json, jsonl]
  -h, --help             Print help
